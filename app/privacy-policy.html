Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Farin English learning application ("the App") handles information.

1. Information We Collect

When you set up your profile, the App collects the name and learning preferences (level, goals, learning style) you enter. This information, along with your learning progress, is saved on your device and also backed up to our database (provided by Google Firebase) under a private recovery code, so you can continue your progress on another device by entering that code.

We do not require you to create an account with a password, and we do not collect your phone number unless you specifically choose to share it (see Section 2).

2. Optional Contact Information

In Settings, you may voluntarily check a box to let the designated teacher message you directly, which requires entering your phone number. This is entirely optional and off by default. If you opt in, your name, phone number, level, and goal are stored so the teacher can reach out to you, primarily via WhatsApp.

You can also choose to contact the teacher yourself at any time through the WhatsApp option in the App, in which case you may voluntarily share your name, level, goals, or any other information you include in your message.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your information may be used to:

  • Save and restore your learning progress across devices;
  • Understand your English level and learning goals;
  • Respond to your questions and, if you opted in, reach out to you directly;
  • Communicate with you about English learning and speaking practice sessions;
  • Provide information about available learning opportunities.

4. Who Can Access Your Information

Your saved profile and progress are only accessible to someone who has your specific recovery code. Contact information you opt in to share (Section 2) is only accessible to the designated teacher, through a password-protected page. We do not sell your personal information.

5. WhatsApp

When you or the teacher initiate contact through WhatsApp, that message and any information shared are sent through WhatsApp, a third-party messaging service operated by Meta.

Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect it, but no method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. Your recovery code should be treated like a password — anyone with it can access the profile it's tied to.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may choose what information to share, and you can turn off direct-contact permission at any time in Settings. If you want your saved data or shared contact information deleted, you may request this directly from the teacher through the WhatsApp contact option in the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the designated teacher through the WhatsApp contact option provided in the App.
