Privacy Policy

Farin English and Farin Spanish (FE School) — last updated November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the FE School apps, Farin English and Farin Spanish ("FE school," "the app"), collect, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It also covers the website thefeschool.com.

The same policy is published on our website at thefeschool.com/privacy-policy.html. The Turkish privacy and cookie policy is at thefeschool.com/gizlilik-politikasi.html.

Information we collect

  • Your name, entered when you set up your profile.
  • A password, chosen next to your name when you create your profile, so you can log in to the same account on another device. The password itself is never stored: the app only keeps a one-way scrambled version of your name and password, used to find your account.
  • Your WhatsApp number, asked when you create your profile. See "Your WhatsApp number" below.
  • Learning profile data: your stated goal (e.g. career, travel, exam prep), your level, and your answers to a short "how do you learn best" question.
  • Progress data: which lessons, stories, and practice rounds you've completed, your streak, and earned badges.
  • Basic usage data, saved with your backup: when you joined, when you last opened the app, on how many days and how many times you've opened it, your device type (for example Android or Windows, installed app or browser) and the app's display language.
  • What you send in Messages: your messages to Farin, course requests and any photos you add, such as a payment receipt. See "Messages, course requests and receipts" below.
  • Course waiting list entries — only if you join a course's waiting list in the app or on the website (thefeschool.com): your name and surname, phone number, the course, the app side, your language, and, for website entries, that it came from the website.
  • Your voice — only when you tap a microphone button. In Speaking Practice and Say It Back, your browser's or device's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text (in Chrome, the audio goes to Google's speech service while you speak). Some course speaking tasks let you record yourself and listen back: that recording stays on your phone, is never uploaded or saved, and is gone when you leave the task.

Your WhatsApp number

  • What we ask for. When you create your profile, the app asks for your WhatsApp number with the country code, and you need it to finish signing up. If you joined before 13 November 2026, the app asks you once in a "Join our club" pop-up, and you can tap "Not now". You can also add or change your number in Settings.
  • Why. So Farin, the teacher who runs FE School, can message you on WhatsApp with news, discounts and help, and about courses. For example, if you join a course's waiting list, he sends you the course link and how to pay. Farin writes these messages himself; the app does not send WhatsApp messages automatically.
  • Where it is stored. On your device, in your account's backup in Google Firebase (Firestore), and in a contact list in the same database, together with your name, level, goal, recovery code, and details such as the app's language and the date. It is also attached to your chats and course requests in Messages, to IELTS writing you send for review, and to the record of any course password you get, so Farin knows who he is talking to.
  • Who sees it. Only Farin, in the app's password-protected admin page. It is never shown to other learners. Like the rest of your profile, it can also be seen by anyone who has your recovery code, or your name and password, so keep those private.
  • Who else gets it. We don't sell it, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't send it to Google Analytics. Google Firebase stores it as our database, and the app's requests to that database pass through our relay on Cloudflare (see "Third-party services"). When Farin messages you, the conversation happens on WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own terms.
  • How long we keep it. As long as your account exists. We don't delete it automatically; we delete it when you ask us to.
  • How to change or delete it. In the app, go to Settings › "Stay in touch": type a new number and tap Save, or untick the box to tell us you don't want news and offers (your number stays saved until you change it or ask us to delete it). To have your number deleted completely, email us at the address below or write to Farin in Messages, with the name you use in the app.

Messages, course requests and receipts

The app has a Messages tab where you can write to Farin. Your messages and his replies are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), together with your name, level, the app's language, your WhatsApp number, a scrambled version of your account code, and your device type and browser details. Only Farin can read them, in the admin page. Your chats come back when you log in on another device.

When you tap "I want this course", your request holds the course, its price, how you will pay, your note if you write one, and the photo of your payment receipt if you add one. The app doesn't take payments: you pay by bank transfer and send the receipt so Farin can check it. Photos are made smaller on your phone before they are sent. Photos in a course request are deleted about 90 days after Farin marks the request done; photos in other chats are deleted about 90 days after they are sent. The text of your chats stays with your account until you ask us to delete it.

Course passwords

When Farin has checked your payment, he creates a password that opens the course, and it can arrive in your Messages. The app keeps a record of it in Google Firebase (Firestore): the course, your name and WhatsApp number, a note if Farin adds one, its start and end dates, whether it is active, and when and on which type of device it was used, with that account's name and recovery code. The password itself is not kept in that record, only a one-way scrambled key made from it; a password sent to you in Messages also stays in that chat. The app checks the record about every 12 hours, so when a password ends or is cancelled the course closes again; your course progress is kept.

How we use this information

  • To personalize which grammar, vocabulary, and practice content you see.
  • To track and display your progress, streaks, and badges.
  • To let you log in to your account on another device or browser with your name and password (or with your recovery code, which still works as a backup) and bring back your whole profile, progress and roadmap.
  • To let Farin message you on WhatsApp with news, discounts, help and course information, using the number you gave (see "Your WhatsApp number").
  • To answer what you send in Messages, check course payments and send you your course password.
  • If you choose to, to connect you with a teacher via WhatsApp — this happens when you tap a "message us" style button, which opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; we don't send anything on your behalf automatically.

Where your data is stored

Your profile and progress are stored locally on your device (browser local storage). Once you finish the first setup, a copy of your profile, progress and roadmap is also backed up automatically to Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service, under a private recovery code. If you chose a password, logging in with your name and password on any device brings back that whole account, and you stay logged in on that device until you tap “Log out” in Settings. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Your WhatsApp number, your Messages chats and photos, and your course password records are also stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), as described in the sections above.

If you tap “Send to Farin for review” on an IELTS writing task, your name, your WhatsApp number, an account ID code, the course and task, and the full text you wrote are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) so that Farin can read it and send you feedback.

If you join a course's waiting list, in the app or on the website, your entry is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) so that we can message you about that course. We only use the number in that entry to message you about this course. Your name and number are not sent to Google Analytics. To have an entry deleted, email us at the address below.

Read-aloud voices

Words, sentences and stories are read aloud by clips recorded in advance with open-source text-to-speech voices. The app downloads these clips from its own website; nothing you type or say is sent anywhere to make them. If a clip is missing, your browser's or device's own voice reads the text instead; some browsers use an online voice service for this.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — cloud backup of your profile, progress and roadmap, plus the record that lets your name and password find your account (a one-way scrambled key, never the password itself), your WhatsApp number and contact entry, your Messages chats and photos, course password records, IELTS writing sent for review, and course waiting list entries.
  • Cloudflare — the app's requests to the database pass through a small relay we run on Cloudflare, so the app also works in countries where Google's services are blocked. The website thefeschool.com is also delivered through Cloudflare.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you tap a WhatsApp button, which opens WhatsApp on your own device, and when Farin messages you on the WhatsApp number you gave.
  • Your browser/device's speech recognition service — only while you use a microphone button in Speaking Practice or Say It Back.
  • YouTube — only when you tap a video in a watch card; the video plays from YouTube, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts — the app loads its fonts from Google's font service.
  • Google Analytics 4 — on the website only; see "Website analytics" below.

We do not use advertising SDKs, and the app does not display ads.

Website analytics (thefeschool.com)

The FE School website (thefeschool.com, not the app) uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, including the app download page (thefeschool.com/get/), only if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner ("Kabul et" on Turkish pages, «قبول» on Persian pages). It then records which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are from (country/city level), the type of device and browser, and whether a contact, course or app link (WhatsApp, email, waiting list, app) was clicked. It uses cookies to tell visits apart. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses, and we do not connect this data to your name, phone number or any app profile. It is not used for advertising. The app itself does not use Google Analytics.

If you decline, nothing is loaded or stored. You can change your choice any time via "Cookie preferences" ("Çerez tercihleri" on Turkish pages) in the website footer. The website's full Turkish privacy and cookie policy is at thefeschool.com/gizlilik-politikasi.html.

Your choices

You can reset your progress at any time from the app's Settings screen, and “Log out” in Settings removes your account from that device. You can change your WhatsApp number in Settings › "Stay in touch". If you'd like your cloud-synced data deleted, including your WhatsApp number and your chats, email us at the address below or write to Farin in Messages, with the name you use in the app (and your recovery code, if you have it).

Private lesson records

If you take private lessons with Farin, the lesson files kept for you (lesson notes and progress records) are stored only on the teacher’s own computer and are not published online. They are kept for 12 months after your last lesson and then deleted. You can ask for them to be deleted earlier by emailing us at the address below.

Children

Farin English is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above.
