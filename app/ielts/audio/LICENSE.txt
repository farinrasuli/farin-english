IELTS course voice clips in this folder (ielts/audio)

These MP3 clips (48 kbps mono) were generated with open-source text-to-speech from the listening scripts,
announcer rubrics and speaking-examiner lines of the Farin English app's IELTS course. Each file name is
ttsKey(voice + " " + text); manifest.json lists the clips that exist.

Kokoro voices (bm_george, bm_lewis, bm_daniel, bm_fable, bf_emma, bf_isabella, bf_alice, bf_lily,
af_sarah, af_nicole, af_heart, am_michael, am_adam, am_puck):
  Kokoro-82M (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), Apache License 2.0.

Piper voices (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper, models from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices):
  en_GB-alba-medium (credit: "Alba"): trained on the Edinburgh "alba" recordings (https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270),
    licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  en_GB-northern_english_male-medium and en_GB-southern_english_female-low: trained on the OpenSLR 83
    "Crowdsourced high-quality UK and Ireland English Dialect speech data set" (Google, https://www.openslr.org/83/),
    licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The clips made with these two voices are shared under the Creative Commons
    Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium: voice "Jenny (Dioco)", dataset https://github.com/dioco-group/jenny-tts-dataset
    (attribution licence, commercial use permitted).

Credits as shown in the app (Privacy Policy, section 10 "Voices and Credits"): Kokoro-82M (Apache License 2.0);
"Jenny (Dioco)"; "Alba" (CC BY 4.0); OpenSLR 83 voices northern_english_male and southern_english_female (CC BY-SA 4.0).
