Answer key — open after you finish
1. are going to book
2. isn't going to stay — negative goes on "be", not "going".
3. Are … going to look for — question order: Are + subject + going to.
4. am going to apply
5. will — decision made right now.
6. am going to — "I've decided" = a plan made earlier.
7. is going to — visible evidence (dark clouds).
8. I am going to call the office. — never drop "am".
9. She is going to buy a new phone. — base form after going to.
10. Are you going to move next year? — base form, not -ing.
