Answer key — open after you finish
1. went / bought — both irregular past simple verbs.
2. am going to start — "going to" needs am/is/are + going to + base verb.
3. better — comparative of "good" is irregular: good → better.
4. the best — superlative of "good" is irregular: good → the best.
5. some / any — "some" in a request (Can I have some…?), "any" in a negative (I don't have any…).
6. was — "be" in the past simple, 1st person singular/3rd person singular.
7. I didn't go to the lesson last week. — no double past after didn't.
8. She is taller than her sister. — don't use "more" and "-er" together.
9. I don't have any homework to do tonight. — "any" belongs in negative sentences.
10. Can I try it on before I buy it? — the pronoun goes in the middle of a phrasal verb.
11. Next week I'm going to visit my cousin. — don't forget am/is/are before "going to".
