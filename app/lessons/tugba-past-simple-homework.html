Answer key — open after you finish
1. watched — regular verb, add -ed.
2. went — go is irregular, "goed" doesn't exist.
3. bought — buy is irregular.
4. didn't come — after didn't, the verb stays in its base form.
5. Did … see — questions use did + base form, even for "see".
6. I didn't go to work yesterday. — no double past after didn't.
7. Did you buy the tickets? — same rule in questions.
8. Last week I called my sister. — verb comes right after the subject (SVO).
9. She studied all night. — consonant + y → ied.
10. I went to the market on Sunday. — go → went.
11. I met her in 2019. → I didn't meet her in 2019.
12. Did he work on Saturday?
