Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Farin English learning application ("the App") handles information shared by users.

1. Information You May Choose to Share

The App does not require users to provide a phone number or create an account.

When you choose to contact the teacher through WhatsApp, you may voluntarily share information such as:

  • Your name;
  • Your English level;
  • Your English learning goals;
  • Information about your learning needs;
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information you voluntarily share through WhatsApp may be used by the designated teacher to:

  • Understand your English level and learning goals;
  • Respond to your questions;
  • Communicate with you about English learning and speaking practice sessions;
  • Provide information about available learning opportunities.

3. Who Can Access Your Information

Information that you voluntarily share through WhatsApp may be accessed by the designated teacher who receives and responds to your message.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. WhatsApp

When you choose to contact the teacher through WhatsApp, your message and any information you choose to share are sent through WhatsApp, a third-party messaging service operated by Meta.

Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

The App does not independently collect or store the information you send through WhatsApp.

5. Information Stored by the App

The App does not require account registration and does not maintain a separate database containing users' names, phone numbers, English levels, or learning goals.

Information voluntarily shared through WhatsApp may be retained within WhatsApp or by the designated teacher as necessary for communication and providing learning-related services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may choose what information to share when contacting the teacher.

If you want information you have shared with the teacher through WhatsApp to be deleted from the teacher's records, you may request this directly from the teacher.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information shared through WhatsApp is handled, please contact the designated teacher through the WhatsApp contact option provided in the App.
