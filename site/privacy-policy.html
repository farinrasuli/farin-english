Privacy Policy

Farin English (FE school) — last updated August 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Farin English app ("FE school," "the app") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information we collect

  • Your name, entered when you set up your profile.
  • Learning profile data: your stated goal (e.g. career, travel, exam prep), your English level, and your answers to a short "how do you learn best" question.
  • Progress data: which lessons, stories, and practice rounds you've completed, your streak, and earned badges.
  • Phone number — only if you choose to request contact with a teacher; this is optional and only used to reach you about lessons.
  • Voice audio — only while actively using the Speaking Practice feature and only if you tap the microphone button. Audio is processed by your device/browser's built-in speech-to-text service to produce a text transcript; the app itself does not record or store audio.

How we use this information

  • To personalize which grammar, vocabulary, and practice content you see.
  • To track and display your progress, streaks, and badges.
  • To let you recover your progress on a new device or browser using your recovery code.
  • If you choose to, to connect you with a teacher for a live session via WhatsApp — this happens only when you tap a "message us" style button, which opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; we don't send anything on your behalf automatically.

Where your data is stored

Your profile and progress are stored locally on your device (browser local storage). If you save a recovery code, a copy of your profile and progress is also stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service, so you can restore it on another device using that code. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — optional cloud backup of your profile/progress, only if you use the recovery-code feature.
  • WhatsApp — only if you choose to tap a "contact a teacher" button, which opens WhatsApp with your own device's WhatsApp app or website.
  • Your browser/device's speech recognition service — only while using the Speaking Practice microphone feature.

We do not use advertising SDKs, and the app does not display ads.

Your choices

You can reset your progress at any time from the app's Settings screen. If you'd like your cloud-synced data (tied to a recovery code) deleted, email us at the address below with your recovery code.

Children

Farin English is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above.
