Privacy Policy

Farin English (FE school) — last updated November 18, 2026 · فارسی · Türkçe

This policy explains what information the FE School apps, Farin English and Farin Spanish ("FE school," "the app"), collect, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It also covers the website thefeschool.com.

Information we collect

  • Your name, entered when you set up your profile.
  • A password, chosen next to your name when you create your profile, so you can log in to the same account on another device. It is kept with your login record in Google Firebase (Firestore), so Farin can help you if you forget it. Only Farin sees it, in the app's password-protected admin page, and the record can only be found with your name and password. Pick a password you don't use anywhere else.
  • Your WhatsApp number, asked when you create your profile. See "Your WhatsApp number" below.
  • Your choice about news and offers: whether you said yes to news, discounts and offers on WhatsApp, and when you made or changed that choice. See "News, discounts and offers on WhatsApp" below.
  • Learning profile data: your stated goal (e.g. career, travel, exam prep), your level, and your answers to a short "how do you learn best" question.
  • Progress data: which lessons, stories, and practice rounds you've completed, your streak, and earned badges.
  • Basic usage data, saved with your backup: when you joined, when you last opened the app, on how many days and how many times you've opened it, your device type (for example Android or Windows, installed app or browser) and the app's display language.
  • What you send in Messages: your messages to Farin, course requests and any photos you add, such as a payment receipt. See "Messages, course requests and receipts" below.
  • Course waiting list entries — only if you join a course's waiting list in the app or on the website (thefeschool.com): your name and surname, phone number, the course, the app side, your language, and, for website entries, that it came from the website.
  • Your voice — only when you tap a microphone button. In Speaking Practice and Say It Back, your browser's or device's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text (in Chrome, the audio goes to Google's speech service while you speak). Some course speaking tasks let you record yourself and listen back: that recording stays on your phone, is never uploaded or saved, and is gone when you leave the task.

Your WhatsApp number

  • What we ask for. When you create your profile, the app asks for your WhatsApp number with the country code, and you need it to finish signing up. Next to it is a separate box for news and offers; it starts unticked and you don't need it to sign up (see "News, discounts and offers on WhatsApp" below). If you joined before 13 November 2026, the app asks you once in a "Join our club" pop-up, and you can tap "Not now". You can also add or change your number in Settings.
  • Why. So Farin, the teacher who runs FE School, can message you on WhatsApp about your account, your courses and any help you need. For example, if you join a course's waiting list, he sends you the course link and how to pay. He only sends you news, discounts and offers if you said yes to them (see "News, discounts and offers on WhatsApp" below). Farin writes these messages himself; the app does not send WhatsApp messages automatically.
  • Where it is stored. On your device, in your account's backup in Google Firebase (Firestore), and in a contact list in the same database, together with your name, level, goal, recovery code, and details such as the app's language and the date. It is also attached to your chats and course requests in Messages, to IELTS writing you send for review, and to the record of any course password you get, so Farin knows who he is talking to.
  • Who sees it. Only Farin, in the app's password-protected admin page. It is never shown to other learners. Like the rest of your profile, it can also be seen by anyone who has your recovery code, or your name and password, so keep those private.
  • Who else gets it. We don't sell it, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't send it to Google Analytics. Google Firebase stores it as our database, and the app's requests to that database pass through our relay on Cloudflare (see "Third-party services"). When Farin messages you, the conversation happens on WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own terms.
  • How long we keep it. As long as your account exists. We don't delete it automatically; we delete it when you ask us to.
  • How to change or delete it. In the app, go to Settings › "Stay in touch", type a new number and tap Save ("Save & send on WhatsApp" in Farin Spanish). The news and offers box is in the same place (see below); unticking it doesn't delete your number. To have your number deleted completely, email us at the address below or write to Farin in Messages, with the name you use in the app.

News, discounts and offers on WhatsApp (only if you say yes)

Farin sends two kinds of WhatsApp messages, and we keep them apart:

  • Account, course and support messages can go to every learner who gave a WhatsApp number. They are about things you have or asked for: answers to your questions, help with your account or a course, the course link and how to pay after you join a course's waiting list or ask for a course, your course password, and important notices about a course you are taking.
  • News, discounts and offers, for example a new course, a discount or a special offer, go only to learners who said yes to them.

Small group class offers. When enough learners with your level and goal could fill a small group class (4 to 6 people), Farin may send you an offer on WhatsApp, but only if you said yes to news and offers, or if you asked for that course yourself. His message links a page on thefeschool.com made for you. It shows your first name, your level and goal, what you told the app you find hard, your group's favourite topics, how many learners match, and the class times and price (the times in Iran or Turkey time, chosen from your phone number's country code). The page reads these from its own link; it saves nothing and uses no cookies or analytics.

How you say yes. It is a separate, optional choice. When you sign up, and in the "Join our club" pop-up, there is a separate box next to the WhatsApp number: "Send me news and offers on WhatsApp". It starts unticked and is never ticked for you; you can sign up, use the app and buy courses without ticking it. You can also tick it later in Settings › "Stay in touch". If you joined before 14 November 2026, you count as not having said yes until you tick the box yourself.

The record of your choice. We keep a record of your choice, when you made it (date and time) and where (at signup, in the "Join our club" pop-up or in Settings), with your account: on your device and in your account's backup in Google Firebase (Firestore). When you change it, the new choice and the time you changed it are recorded. Farin sees your choice in the app's admin page and sends news and offers only to learners who said yes.

How to stop them at any time. In the app, go to Settings › "Stay in touch" and untick "Send me news and offers on WhatsApp". Or write to Farin in Messages, or email us at the address below, and say you don't want news and offers any more; from then on we don't send you any. Stopping is free and changes nothing else: you keep your account and your courses, you still get account, course and support messages, and your number stays saved until you change it or ask us to delete it. You can say yes again in the same place whenever you like.

Messages, course requests and receipts

The app has a Messages tab where you can write to Farin. Your messages and his replies are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), together with your name, level, the app's language, your WhatsApp number, a scrambled version of your account code, and your device type and browser details. Only Farin can read them, in the admin page. Your chats come back when you log in on another device.

Offers from Farin in Messages. Farin may start a chat with you in Messages about one of our courses that fits your main goal and level, for example Job Interview English if you chose a career goal. It comes with a button to see the course, and if notifications are on, your phone shows it. It never goes to someone who already has that course, or to someone who turned off "Course news and offers" in Settings › "Notifications"; you can also write to Farin in that chat and ask him to stop. So that the chat shows up in your Messages, the app keeps a small mailbox in Google Firebase (Firestore), under a scrambled key made from your account code, which holds only the chats Farin started for you. The chat holds the same details as your other chats (your name, level, the app's language, your WhatsApp number and a scrambled version of your account code). The admin page keeps a record of which course offer it sent you, when, and how (in the app or on WhatsApp), with a scrambled version of your account code and no name or number, so the same offer isn't sent to you twice.

When you tap "I want this course", your request holds the course, its price, how you will pay, your note if you write one, and the photo of your payment receipt if you add one. The app doesn't take payments: you pay by bank transfer and send the receipt so Farin can check it. Photos are made smaller on your phone before they are sent. Photos in a course request are deleted about 90 days after Farin marks the request done; photos in other chats are deleted about 90 days after they are sent. The text of your chats stays with your account until you ask us to delete it.

Course passwords

When Farin has checked your payment, he creates a password that opens the course, and it can arrive in your Messages. The app keeps a record of it in Google Firebase (Firestore): the course, your name and WhatsApp number, a note if Farin adds one, its start and end dates, whether it is active, and when and on which type of device it was used, with that account's name and recovery code. The password itself is not kept in that record, only a one-way scrambled key made from it; a password sent to you in Messages also stays in that chat. The app checks the record about every 12 hours, so when a password ends or is cancelled the course closes again; your course progress is kept.

Phone notifications

Signing up turns notifications on: when you sign up, your phone asks you once to allow them (if it doesn't ask, or you say not now, the app reminds you once to turn them on), and if your phone already allows them they come on without a question. You can turn them off any time in Settings › "Notifications". When they are on, your phone or browser gives the app a push address, and our small notification server on Cloudflare keeps it with your choices: a scrambled version of your account code, the app and the language your notifications come in (the app's language, or Persian if you use the app in English with an Iranian number; the number itself is not sent), your level and goal, the courses you have, your first name, your time zone, the hour you usually use the app, and the reminders the app plans for you (for example your next card or a game). Notifications reach your device through its own push service (Google for Android and Chrome, Apple for iPhone, Mozilla for Firefox, Microsoft for Edge), encrypted on the way so only your device can read them.

What you get. Farin's answers and course passwords from Messages; announcements Farin sends to a group of learners; and, if you keep reminders on, a short reminder when you haven't opened the app for a while (at most one a day, never between 22:00 and 10:00).

Course news and offers. With notifications on, you also get occasional news about our courses and offers. They come to everyone with notifications on; you can turn them off any time in Settings › "Notifications" › "Course news and offers", and we record that choice and its date with your account. This is separate from the WhatsApp news and offers above, which come only if you say yes.

Offers for your goal. Some notifications go to a group of learners: those whose main goal, level and app language fit, for example career news for learners who chose a career goal. A course offer never goes to someone who already has that course. Some are planned ahead and sent automatically: at most about once a week for your group, never between 22:00 and 10:00, and not if you got another notification in the last 20 hours. The notification server only counts how many were sent and opened for each message, not who opened it.

Announcements in the app. Farin's announcements are also shown in the app, once as a pop-up and in Messages under "News from Farin". The app downloads the recent list from the same server; nothing about you is sent for this.

How to stop. Untick the boxes in Settings › "Notifications", or block notifications for the app in your phone's settings. Turning notifications off deletes your push address from the server, and addresses that stop working are deleted automatically. The server counts how many notifications are sent and opened, without recording who opened them.

How we use this information

  • To personalize which grammar, vocabulary, and practice content you see.
  • To track and display your progress, streaks, and badges.
  • To let you log in to your account on another device or browser with your name and password (or with your recovery code, which still works as a backup) and bring back your whole profile, progress and roadmap.
  • To let Farin message you on WhatsApp about your account, your courses and help, using the number you gave, and, only if you said yes, with news, discounts and offers (see "News, discounts and offers on WhatsApp").
  • To answer what you send in Messages, check course payments and send you your course password.
  • If you choose to, to connect you with a teacher via WhatsApp — this happens when you tap a "message us" style button, which opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; we don't send anything on your behalf automatically.

Where your data is stored

Your profile and progress are stored locally on your device (browser local storage). Once you finish the first setup, a copy of your profile, progress and roadmap is also backed up automatically to Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service, under a private recovery code. If you chose a password, logging in with your name and password on any device brings back that whole account, and you stay logged in on that device until you tap “Log out” in Settings. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Your WhatsApp number, your choice about news and offers, your Messages chats and photos, and your course password records are also stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), as described in the sections above.

If you tap “Send to Farin for review” on an IELTS writing task, your name, your WhatsApp number, an account ID code, the course and task, and the full text you wrote are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) so that Farin can read it and send you feedback.

If you join a course's waiting list, in the app or on the website, your entry is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) so that we can message you about that course. We only use the number in that entry to message you about this course. Your name and number are not sent to Google Analytics. To have an entry deleted, email us at the address below.

Read-aloud voices

Words, sentences and stories are read aloud by clips recorded in advance with open-source text-to-speech voices. The app downloads these clips from its own website; nothing you type or say is sent anywhere to make them. If a clip is missing, your browser's or device's own voice reads the text instead; some browsers use an online voice service for this.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — cloud backup of your profile, progress and roadmap, plus the record that lets your name and password find your account (with the password itself, which only Farin sees), your WhatsApp number and contact entry, your choice about news and offers, your Messages chats and photos, course password records, IELTS writing sent for review, and course waiting list entries.
  • Cloudflare — the app's requests to the database pass through a small relay we run on Cloudflare, so the app also works in countries where Google's services are blocked. The website thefeschool.com is also delivered through Cloudflare.
  • Push services (Google, Apple, Mozilla, Microsoft) — only if you turn notifications on in the app: they deliver notifications to your device. Our notification server on Cloudflare sends them; see "Phone notifications".
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you tap a WhatsApp button, which opens WhatsApp on your own device, and when Farin messages you on the WhatsApp number you gave.
  • Your browser/device's speech recognition service — only while you use a microphone button in Speaking Practice or Say It Back.
  • YouTube — only when you tap a video in a watch card; the video plays from YouTube, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts — the app loads its fonts from Google's font service.
  • Google Analytics 4 — on the website only; see "Website analytics" below.

We do not use advertising SDKs, and the app does not display ads.

Website analytics (thefeschool.com)

The FE School website (thefeschool.com, not the app) uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, including the app download page (thefeschool.com/get/), only if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner ("Kabul et" on Turkish pages, «قبول» on Persian pages). It then records which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are from (country/city level), the type of device and browser, and whether a contact, course or app link (WhatsApp, email, waiting list, app) was clicked. It uses cookies to tell visits apart. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses, and we do not connect this data to your name, phone number or any app profile. It is not used for advertising. The app itself does not use Google Analytics.

If you decline, nothing is loaded or stored. You can change your choice any time via "Cookie preferences" ("Çerez tercihleri" on Turkish pages, «تنظیمات کوکی» on Persian pages) in the website footer. The website's full Turkish privacy and cookie policy is at thefeschool.com/gizlilik-politikasi.html, and this policy in Persian is at thefeschool.com/fa/privacy.html.

Your choices

You can reset your progress at any time from the app's Settings screen, and “Log out” in Settings removes your account from that device. You can change your WhatsApp number, and turn news and offers on or off, in Settings › "Stay in touch". You can turn notifications, reminders and course news on or off in Settings › "Notifications". If you'd like your cloud-synced data deleted, including your WhatsApp number and your chats, email us at the address below or write to Farin in Messages, with the name you use in the app (and your recovery code, if you have it).

Private lesson records

If you take private lessons with Farin, the lesson files kept for you (lesson notes and progress records) are stored only on the teacher’s own computer and are not published online. They are kept for 12 months after your last lesson and then deleted. You can ask for them to be deleted earlier by emailing us at the address below.

Children

Farin English is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
